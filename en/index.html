Mafia game for companies

Over the club’s eleven years our format has changed, and it is still evolving, with one aim: that guests feel safe and have fun, and that the experience inspires them to trust their feelings. The emotional intelligence the game develops lets participants deepen their connection and build closer relationships that can last beyond the event.

Founder of the club

Since 2009 Oleg Čeklu has been organising and hosting events, writing an original script for each. Since 2011 he has been deepening his knowledge of practices that improve mental and physical health. In 2015 he founded Lithuania’s first Mafia club. In 2026 he created and launched his own platform, “Mafia Online”.
