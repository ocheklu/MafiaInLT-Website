How a Mafia game evening unfolds

  1. Guests arrive

    30 min

    Guests gather over snacks and light drinks.

  2. Dinner

    ~40 min

    Guests have dinner.

  3. Programme starts

    ~15 min

    We invite each guest to the table individually, highlighting their authentic traits.

  4. First session

    60 min

    We explain the rules before the start and remind players what each role does during the session.

  5. Break

    ~15 min

    We leave time to share impressions.

  6. Second and third sessions

    ~40 min each

    We invite those who have not played yet and those who were eliminated first.

  7. Closing

    ~15 min

    The host recalls the evening’s standout moments and presents the guests with nominations.

Organising the game starts at 600 €, and organising the whole event starts at 1 500 €. The final price depends on the number of game tables and the extra services you choose.

In our format, one table seats 10 players. With two tables, 20 people play at once, and across three game sessions we can host up to 60 guests.

These are two different service packages.

Game organisation — we come to the place you choose at the agreed time with our props and table. We run the game.

Event organisation — full planning and coordination of the event, from concept to execution. All we need from you is the date, the time and the number of guests. The service covers: finding and booking the venue; building the programme; liaising with suppliers; finalising the menu; bringing the decor concept to life; coordinating the programme during the event and running the game itself.
